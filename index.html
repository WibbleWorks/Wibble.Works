About Wibble.Works

I'm Chris Edwards — 25 years from developer to CTO to Director of Product, now leading patient-data platforms in pharma. I turn regulated data estates from gated silos into governed, self-service platforms: I architect them, I own them as products, and I build them hands-on.

Rollouts cut from months to hours, teams built from zero, platforms adopted enterprise-wide — always measured by customer happiness. Wibble.Works is how I bring that to pharma R&D, IT data teams and NHS data leaders.

Recent platform leadership includes governed self-service analytics, a next-generation DataLake, partner data exchange, GxP audit tooling, and patient-level consent management — delivered in-house at enterprise scale.
